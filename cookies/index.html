Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This page explains the cookies and similar browser storage used by henriaudio.com and the Writers Only app. The short version: there isn't much to tell.

This website uses no cookies. No analytics, no ad trackers, no tracking pixels, and nothing from social networks. We don't follow you around the web, and there's no cookie banner because there's nothing to agree to.

What this website stores

Nothing. Browsing henriaudio.com doesn't save cookies or anything else in your browser, and every page loads from our own site with no outside services watching.

If you join a waitlist, your email address is sent to our database provider (Supabase) so we can tell you when the thing you asked about is ready. That's a one time form submission. It doesn't place a cookie, and it's covered by our Privacy Policy.

If you arrive from one of our links on Instagram or TikTok, the web address may include a small tag (like ?ref=instagram). If you then join the waitlist, we note which link brought you. That tag lives in the address bar only. It isn't a cookie and it isn't stored in your browser.

What the Writers Only app stores

When you sign in to Writers Only on the web, your browser keeps a sign in token so you stay logged in between visits. This is strictly necessary for the app to work, it is never used for advertising or tracking, and it's removed when you sign out. The app may also remember simple preferences on your device so it opens the way you left it.

Our iPhone app keeps the same kind of sign in information securely on your phone. It doesn't use advertising IDs or cross app tracking.

Third party cookies

We don't use any. If that ever changes, for example if we add analytics, we'll update this page first and, where the law requires it, ask for your permission before anything is set.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and site data any time in your browser's settings. Clearing data for the Writers Only app will sign you out, but your songs are saved to your account and will be there when you sign back in.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update the date above.
