Utility Deck

The control panel that lives on every track.

One window for the moves you make on every track: quick tone shaping and filters, three-dimensional placement — front to back, wide to narrow, left to right — and PUSH, the magic better button. Everything is built for quick, easy automation. Zero latency. No phase issues.

One email with your download link, then a note when Deck updates. Nothing else.

Utility Deck 0.4 — actual interface
Quick tone & filters
Two broad, musical shelves and smooth high-pass and low-pass filters. Shape a source in seconds — and automate every move cleanly.
Place sounds in 3D
Depth, width, and pan work together: push a sound to the back of the song, open it wide, sit it left or right. Or just drag the dot on the little stage.
PUSH
The magic better button. One press and the source sits fuller and clearer. Nothing to set — it adjusts to your material automatically, quiet or loud.
Zero latency, no phase issues
Safe on every track, while recording, and on the master. Width and bass mono are phase-safe by design, so your mix folds to mono without surprises. Presets and A/B compare built in.
